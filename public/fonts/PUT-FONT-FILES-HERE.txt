Put your two Requiem font files in THIS folder:

    RequiemText-HTF-Roman.woff2
    RequiemText-HTF-Italic.woff2

They'll be served at /fonts/RequiemText-HTF-Roman.woff2 (and -Italic),
which is exactly what the @font-face rules in src/App.jsx point to.

Grab them from your WordPress media library, or from:
    https://byrnecreative.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/RequiemText-HTF-Roman.woff2
    https://byrnecreative.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/RequiemText-HTF-Italic.woff2

Keep the filenames as-is. You can delete this .txt once the fonts are in.
